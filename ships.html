Fleet Comparison

The charts above provide a high-level comparison of the fleet. The Hauling chart shows the trade-off between Top Speed and Cargo Capacity (bubble size shows Cargo Throughput), the Acceleration chart ranks how quickly each sailing ship gets up to speed (its sail force), and the Performance chart blends Top Speed and Acceleration together (bubble size shows Agility) for a fuller picture of how a ship handles.

Every stat except top speed is the ship's real in-game value, read from Valheim's and OdinShipPlus's own data. Mass is shown for reference only: the game scales every force on a ship by its mass, so a heavier ship is no slower to start, no slower at full sail and no harder to turn. Top speeds are for full wind at the best angle, 64° off the stern; in a moderate breeze every ship is slower. Speeds marked ✓ were worked out from logged sailing (Karve, Longship, Cargo Ship, Fast Ship Skuldelev, Fast Karve). The rest are estimated from sail force and drag, scaled from the Karve or the Longship.

Key Information

This guide covers the vanilla ships alongside those added by the OdinShipPlus and TheGreatestShips mods. The Source column shows where each ship comes from.

OdinShipPlus: Custom Materials & Crafting Station

The OdinShipPlus mod introduces its own materials like Resin Wood, Caulked Wood, Sail Canvas, and Marine Rope. You must craft these components at the new Carpenter Table station before you can build the ships themselves.

TheGreatestShips: Variants of Vanilla Hulls

The Fast Karve, Fast Longship and Cargo Longship are built at the Workbench from vanilla materials. Each keeps its base ship's health and mass but changes top speed, storage and hull width.

Remember to check the mod's configuration files in your `BepInEx/config` folder for the most precise and up-to-date stats, as these can sometimes be customized by server administrators or updated by the mod author.
