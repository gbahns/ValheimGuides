The Bog Witch

Everything she sells — and what it unlocks.

Where to find her

The Bog Witch was added in patch 0.219.13. She spawns in a hut marked by a Witch Stone — look for a cauldron icon on the map once you're close. Up to 10 possible locations are picked per world during generation, always in Swamp biomes, at least 1000m apart from each other, and between roughly 3000–8000m from the world center. She sells everything for Coins only — no trade-in items or bartering. Several of her wares stay locked until you defeat the boss noted in the "Unlocks / Enables" column below.
